SENTINEL HIMALAYAS

A live disaster detection & response monitor

This simulates a real cloudburst/GLOF event from Sikkim (October 2023) alongside a hypothetical earthquake — both playing out on the same 5-day timeline. Sensor readings feed real statistical detection models that decide, on their own, when a disaster is happening. Once detected, a Case opens and tracks three response phases: Detection, Search & Connectivity, and Relief Delivery — all driven by real generated schedules, not scripted animation.

Everything you see — probabilities, relay deployments, survivor counts, delivery weights — is computed from the underlying data, not staged.
